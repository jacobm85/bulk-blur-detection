2. Scan

Scores every photo in this folder and its subfolders. Only new or changed photos are scored again, so re-running it after adding photos is quick. Nothing is moved.

3. Review

Photos below the threshold are shown blurriest first. Lower score = blurrier. Everything is marked Move; click the photos you want to Keep, then apply. Moved photos (with Live Photo videos, RAW files and sidecars) go to a Blurry folder next to them, and can be undone. Photos with a clear face are judged on the sharpness of the eyes against the eye threshold.

Shift-click a photo (or press B in the large view) to Keep blurry: it stays where it is, but counts as blurry in the training data.

A random sample of photos with any score, to collect training data that also covers blurry photos the score misses. Everything is marked Keep; click the blurry ones to Move them, then apply.
